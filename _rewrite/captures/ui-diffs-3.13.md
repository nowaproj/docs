## Notes from the signed-in captures (web app, Starter plan; not label differences)

- The **New project** arrow menu opens over the Projects header: it covers the grid/list switch and the label of the **New project** button
  (`account-projects-1`). The card ⋮ menu opens above its button and covers part of the card's name (`account-projects-2`).
- **Starter** plan behavior seen in the UI, as the pages already describe it: **Premium** pills on the mobile rows of the **Deploy** menu (click: **Time to level up**,
  "Mobile deployment is available on paid plans. Upgrade to deploy."), a lock with **Upgrade** on the **Android** and **iOS** tabs ("Android builds are not available on your current plan."),
  **Clone from GitHub** and **Compress Project** open **Time to level up**, and the **Git** panel and **Git** settings show "Your plan does not support git integration".
- The embedded preview (**Run**) starts the cloud run service but its app never connected in the capture sandbox ("No client connected" in the **Logs** tab):
  the debug connection needs a WebSocket, which the sandbox's network proxy does not allow. The run toolbar and the **Scan the QR** dropdown still work (`test-run-1`).
