## Shift + drag: what the two live runs support

`captures/phase12/c.md` saw a Shift + drag drop the widget out of its screen. `P12-live-checks.md` item 5 found that the
widget stays in its screen unless the pointer is outside the screen at the drop, and then it leaves even though it still
looks inside; one Ctrl/Cmd + Z puts it back; Shift + an arrow key moves it 10 px. The code agrees with item 5: during a
drag `MoveTool.update` finds the drop target from the raw pointer (`packages/designer/lib/src/design_experience/move_tool.dart:116-127`,
`_findHost(to)` at `:255-274`, `hitTestCanvas` uses `board.pointerLocation` at
`packages/designer/lib/src/design_experience/designer_board_controller.dart:34-35`), while the drawn spot is the
axis-locked `_mapSingleAxis` (`move_tool.dart:174-183`, Shift read at `designer_board_controller.dart:198-200`). So both
pages now say: Shift + drag *can* drop the widget out of its screen; it happens when the pointer is outside the screen at
release, even if the widget looks inside; one undo restores it; Shift + arrow nudges 10 px (`packages/designer/lib/src/designer_setup.dart:26-49`).
c.md's run is consistent with this (it recorded no pointer position), so nothing says "always" or "never".
