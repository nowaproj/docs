## Open issues

1. **Google side is described less than Apple's.** The Payments policy, User Data policy, account deletion requirements and Policy Center pages are only linked, never described, because `play.google.com` and `support.google.com` cannot be fetched here. If someone can read them, the Android section could say precisely what Google requires, as the iOS section does. Their link targets are confirmed only through search-result titles (Payments policy, User Data, Policy Center) and, for the account deletion page, through the September and October 2023 Android Developers newsletters that link it. Re-check the four URLs and titles when access exists.
2. **RevenueCat and "restore purchases".** Apple 3.1.1 says you should have a restore mechanism.
