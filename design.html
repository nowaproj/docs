How designing works

In Nowa you design on a board: one big, free-form space where your screens sit side by side. Click anything to change it, drag to rearrange, and let Nowa AI build alongside you. Your screens and components become part of a real app that you own.

How the pieces fit​

A board holds board items. A board item is a screen, a component or a loose widget. Each screen and component is made of widgets, the building blocks of your app, nested inside each other: a card holds a picture and a title, a screen holds the cards.

PieceWhat it isIn your app
BoardA free-form design space. A project can have many.Not part of your app. It's your workspace.
ScreenOne page of your app, such as Home or Settings.A page people can open.
ComponentA widget you build once and reuse on many screens, such as a recipe card.Part of every screen that uses it.
Loose widgetAny widget you drop straight onto the board, such as a shape.Nothing yet, until you place it inside a screen.
WidgetA building block inside a screen or component: a Text, a Button, a Column.Part of the screen or component it sits in.

Screens and components have a title bar above them with their name. Hover it to see Play and Open in new tab. A loose widget has no title bar, which is a quick way to tell it apart.

The tools you work with​

WhereWhat it does
Library panel, left sidebarLists your screens and components, Nowa's built-in widgets, your packages and your assets. Search it, or drag a row onto the board. See Find and add things with the Library.
Outline panel, left sidebarShows how the widgets nest inside each other, so you can jump to any widget, even one that's hidden or hard to click.
Toolbar, bottom of the boardSelect tool, Shape, Screen, Text and Widget.
Details, top rightThe settings of whatever is selected: its color, size, text and more. With nothing selected, it shows the board's color and grid.
Variables, above DetailsThe values the selected screen or component receives and remembers, and the actions it can run (its params, variables and functions). With nothing selected, it shows Globals: data every screen of your app can use.

The board is part of the desktop-width layout. On a phone-sized window Nowa shows a simpler view without a board, described in Use Nowa on your phone.

Design with Nowa AI and by hand​

The AI Assistant panel is open next to the board when you open a project. Describe a screen and Nowa AI builds it. When it creates a screen, it places it on your board, so you see the result right away. Then refine by hand: select a widget and change it in Details.

The two work well together. The widget you select on the board is attached to your next message automatically, so you can point at exactly what you want changed. See Give Nowa AI context.

Or ask Nowa AI

Try "Create a Home screen with a search bar and a list of recipes." Then select the search bar on the board and adjust it yourself.

Try it as you go​

Hover a screen's title and click Play to tap through it right on the board. It's the quickest way to check a flow while you design. See Play your app on the board.
