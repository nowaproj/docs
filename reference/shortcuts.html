Keyboard shortcuts

Keep your hands on the keyboard while you build: add a widget, group a few, run your app or undo a mistake without reaching for the mouse. This page lists the editor's shortcuts, grouped by where you use them. You don't need to learn them all at once. Start with these five:

  • Ctrl/Cmd + K opens the Library so you can add a widget.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes your last change.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + G groups the selected widgets.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + P runs your app in the Embedded preview.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + . shows the Shortcuts sheet, in case you forget the rest.

Most shortcuts use Ctrl on Windows and Linux and Cmd on macOS, so the tables below list both. On a Mac, tooltips and menus show Control as ⌃, Option as ⌥, Shift as ⇧ and Cmd as ⌘, always in that order, as in ⇧⌘Z or ⌥⌘]. On Windows and Linux they show Ctrl, Alt and ⇧ in the same order. You can't change the shortcuts.

tip

Hover a board tool, or open a right-click menu, to see its shortcut next to the name.

Open the Shortcuts sheet​

The editor has a built-in cheat sheet with common shortcuts, handy while you're still learning them.

  1. Click the keyboard icon (Shortcuts) at the bottom of the left sidebar, or press Ctrl + . (Cmd + . on macOS). The sheet opens over the editor.
  2. Close it with the same keys, the close button, Esc or a click outside the sheet.

The sheet shows its shortcuts in four groups, General, Tab Actions, Widgets and Designer, and leaves out many that are on this page. Tab Actions holds Back, Forward and Boards. Designer holds Add a widget and Go to a widget. Widgets holds Bring forward, Send backward, Bring to front and Send to back.

Two entries don't match what the keys really do:

Sheet entryThe sheet showsWhat happens
Show/Hide panelsCtrl/Cmd + \Not bound to anything. To open or close a sidebar panel, use Ctrl/Cmd and a number, as in Sidebar panels.
Group/UngroupCtrl/Cmd + GIt only groups. To ungroup, right-click the group and choose Ungroup.

The Library has no Delete key: right-click a row and choose Delete. Back and Forward step through the editors you opened and keep your last 50 places. So undoing in Circuit doesn't touch your design on the board. Action History lists the changes of that area. Click an entry to undo it and every change after it.

Run your app​

ActionWindows / LinuxmacOS
Run your app in the Embedded preview (from a board or an open screen or component)Ctrl + PCmd + P
Restart the app while the Embedded preview is openShift + RShift + R
Show the app fullscreen while the Embedded preview is openCtrl + FCmd + F

The restart key does what the restart button in the top bar does: it puts your latest saved changes into the running app. In a local project that's Hot Reload, which updates the app in place. In a cloud project it's Hot Restart, which starts the app again. Instant Play has no shortcut: hover a screen's title and click Play. See Run your app and Play your app on the board.

When a shortcut does nothing​

  • Focus. Designer shortcuts work when the board or Outline has focus. Click the board or an item on it first.
  • Typing. While you type in a field, the tool keys (V, R, T), F, the arrow keys, [ and ], select all, copy, cut, paste, remove, undo and redo are ignored, so they don't change your design by accident.
  • Playing. Designer shortcuts are off while an item plays on the board. Click Stop, then try again.
  • View only. If you have the View Only role, only copy, the two tab-switching keys and Ctrl/Cmd + W work. Panning and zooming still work.
  • Pop-ups. Ctrl/Cmd + O and Ctrl/Cmd + K don't open while the Shortcuts sheet or a picker is already open.
  • Browser. A browser keeps some combinations for itself, such as Ctrl/Cmd + W, so a few shortcuts may not reach the web app. Use the buttons instead.

Two exceptions in Nowa 3.13 go the other way: a / you type in a Details field doesn't appear, and while you name something in the Variables list, the letters t, r, v and f can trigger board shortcuts instead of typing. Known issues has a workaround for each: the / in Details and the letters in the Variables list.
