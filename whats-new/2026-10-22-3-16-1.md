Nowa 3.16.1 lets you try your app the moment you change it. 🚀 It plays right inside the editor with no build and no waiting, so you can test it, fix what you see and get feedback on it without ever leaving your project.

<!-- truncate -->

## ▶️ Try your app instantly

Click the arrow next to **Run** and choose **Instant play**. Your app starts right where your board was, so you can tap through your screens, fill in your forms and check every flow the way your users will. There's nothing to build, so it's ready in a second. In the playground and in templates, just click **Run**.

- **Check every screen size**: switch between **Phone**, **Tablet**, **Desktop** and **Full screen** in the top bar.
- **Start fresh**: click **Start over** to run your app from the beginning, as if you had just opened it.
- **Try one screen on its own**: click the screen's name next to the size, and pick the screen you want to play.
- **Hop back to your board** with the back arrow in the top bar. Forward takes you right back to your app.

## 🎯 Fix what you see, while it plays

Spotted something that's off? Click **Select** in the top bar, or press **V**, and click it in your running app. Nowa outlines it just like on your board. Open **Details** beside your app to change its text, color or spacing, and watch your app update as you type. Hold **Shift** to pick a few things at once, and press **Esc** to go back to using your app.

Nowa AI sees what you pick, too. Select a button, ask the **Assistant** to "make this button bigger", and it knows exactly which button you mean.

## 💬 Get feedback right on your app

Click **Comment**, or press **C**, then click anywhere on your app to leave a note exactly where it belongs. For a second opinion, click **Share** to copy a link to your app. The people on your project can open it in their browser, try your app and add their own comments, and if your project is public, anyone with the link can try it.

Every comment stays in your project, so nothing gets lost in chats or emails. **Resolve** a comment once it's handled, or click **Fix with AI** and let Nowa AI make the change it asks for.

## 🗂️ All your data in one panel

**Api**, **Firestore** and **Supabase** now live together in the new **Data** panel in the left sidebar, one tab each, and it opens on the tab you used last. Every tab works the same way: search to find what you need, connect right from the tab when something isn't connected yet, and point at an API request or a Supabase query or function to run it straight from the list.

![The Data panel in the left sidebar, with its Api, Firestore and Supabase tabs, the search field, and the Run request button on a request highlighted](/img/whats_new/3.16.1-data-panel.png)

## 🧭 Help, right where you look for it

Help, your notifications and your account now sit together at the bottom of the left sidebar. Click **?** to read the docs, message our support team, look up a keyboard shortcut or book a call with a Nowa expert. Your project settings live down there too, and they open in a popup, so you never lose your place.

Looking for the Router? Select a screen and click the route icon (**Open Router Editor**) under **Route Settings** in its **Details**.

## 🤖 Nowa AI does more with your credits

- **Longer chats for fewer credits**: Nowa AI now reads your project's overview, and any large screen or component you attach, only when your request needs it, instead of carrying it in every message. Long chats on big projects use noticeably fewer credits.
- **Your theme stays yours**: when you design with Nowa AI in an app you imported, it styles your screens with your app's own theme instead of adding a new one.
- **Packages without the extra step**: ask Nowa AI to add a package and it adds it, with no experimental setting to turn on first.
