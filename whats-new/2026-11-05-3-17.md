With Nowa 3.17, you can run several AI chats at once, and Git now works on every plan. 🚀

<!-- truncate -->

## 💬 Run several AI chats at once

You no longer have to wait for Nowa AI to finish one thing before you start the next. Leave a chat while it works and it keeps going in the background, so you can ask for a new screen while another request is still building.

- **Start another chat anytime.** Click **+** (**New chat**) at the top of the AI panel, even while Nowa AI is busy.
- **See what's still working.** The **Chat history** icon shows a dot while other chats run, and pointing at it tells you how many. In **All Sessions**, the chats that are still working are marked **Running**.
- **Add to a request while it runs.** Type a message while Nowa AI is working and it waits under the field, marked **Queued**, until Nowa AI picks it up at its next step. Changed your mind? Click **×** to remove it. If the request stops before then, your message goes back into the field.

Chats take turns changing your project, and each one keeps its own checkpoints. See [Chat with Nowa AI](/ai/chat).

## 🌿 Git for every plan

Keep a history of your app on any plan. Open the **Git** panel to see what changed, compare versions, commit, discard what you don't want, browse your history, and undo or revert a commit. Connecting GitHub to push, pull and work with branches is still part of the paid plans. See [Use Git](/code/git).

Git also keeps up better with the rest of your work. Files that arrive with a pull, a branch switch or a merge show up right away, and when a lot changes at once, or something changes outside Nowa, Nowa reloads the project once and tells you why. It never does that while you have unsaved edits.

## 📊 Credits you can follow

- **A meter that keeps up.** The usage meter under the chat now moves after every step Nowa AI takes, in every chat that's running, instead of only when a request finishes.
- **Deep Thinking goes the distance.** On smaller plans, Deep Thinking could stop after its first steps and say your budget was almost used up, even with credits left. Now it keeps working until your credits really run out.
- **Back as soon as you are.** When your credits run out, the chat tells you when they reset. After the reset or a top-up, **Send** comes back by itself, and the same message says your credits are available again, with **Continue** to pick up where Nowa AI left off.

See [Plans, billing and AI usage](/account/plans-and-usage#usage).
