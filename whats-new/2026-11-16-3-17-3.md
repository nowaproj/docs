With Nowa 3.17.3, you can run several AI chats at once and choose how hard Nowa AI thinks from one menu. Every cloud build now uses the exact setup your version of Nowa was tested with. 🚀

<!-- truncate -->

## 💬 Run several AI chats at once

You no longer have to wait for Nowa AI to finish one thing before you start the next. If you leave a chat while it works, it keeps going in the background, so you can ask for a new screen while another request is still building.

- **Start another chat anytime.** Click **+** (**New chat**) at the top of the AI panel, even while Nowa AI is busy.
- **See what's still working.** The **Chat history** icon shows a dot while other chats are running. Click it to see your chats. The ones still working show a spinner.
- **Add to a request while it runs.** A message you type while Nowa AI is working waits at the top of the chat field, marked **Queued**, until Nowa AI picks it up at its next step. Click **×** to take it back.

Chats take turns changing your project, and each one keeps its own checkpoints. See [Chat with Nowa AI](/ai/chat).

## 🎚️ Pick the mode and how hard Nowa AI thinks, in one place

The mode chip under the chat field now shows both choices, like **Agent · Thinking**. Click it to pick **Design**, **Plan** or **Agent**, then drag the slider under them to choose **Instant** for quick edits, **Thinking** for most requests or **Deep Thinking** for big features and tricky logic. The same menu is in the dashboard's **What do you want to build?** box, and Plan mode now gets a thinking level too. See [Design, Plan and Agent modes](/ai/modes#set-the-thinking-level).

Nowa AI can now also edit your app's `Info.plist` and `AndroidManifest.xml`. Ask it for something your app needs from the phone, like "keep playing music in the background", and it adds the settings for you.

## 🧰 Builds on a setup tested with your Nowa

Each Nowa release now comes with the exact Flutter, Java and Xcode versions it was tested with, and your cloud builds use them. Your app builds in the cloud with the same Flutter that runs it in Nowa, and a new Flutter or Xcode reaches your project with a Nowa update, when it's been tested, never by surprise. Nowa also leaves your Android files as they are, unless they're too old to build.

Your apps get lighter too. The **Web View** now lives only in the apps that use it. If yours does, Nowa asks once to move it into your project, with a **Web View update** notice that lists each change. Click **Migrate**, and your screens look and work the same.

## 🪵 All your logs in one place

When something goes wrong, you can now find out why without leaving the **Logs** panel. It collects your app's output wherever it runs: on the board and in Instant play (**App in Nowa**), in the **Run** preview (**Nowa Run**) and in a **Local run** on your computer. It also shows what happens when you publish to the web (**Web publish**), and it keeps Nowa's own messages apart. Use the filter at the top (**Show: All sources**) to look at just one.

- **A preview that says why it stopped.** If your app runs out of memory in the **Run** preview, Nowa says "Your app needs more memory", and an app that crashes says it stopped unexpectedly, instead of showing a general error.
- **Versions at a glance.** Point at the version in the status bar to see which Flutter and Dart Nowa runs, and click it for more. For a local project, it also tells you if Flutter is missing on your computer or older than Nowa's, and the version turns amber when something needs a look.
