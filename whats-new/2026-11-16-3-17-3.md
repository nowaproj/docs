With Nowa 3.17.3, you can run several AI chats at once and choose how hard Nowa AI thinks from one menu. Git now works on every plan, and your cloud builds keep working when Flutter releases a new version. 🚀

<!-- truncate -->

## 💬 Run several AI chats at once

You no longer have to wait for Nowa AI to finish one thing before you start the next. If you leave a chat while it works, it keeps going in the background, so you can ask for a new screen while another request is still building.

- **Start another chat anytime.** Click **+** (**New chat**) at the top of the AI panel, even while Nowa AI is busy.
- **See what's still working.** The **Chat history** icon shows a dot while other chats are running. Click it to see your chats. The ones still working show a spinner.
- **Add to a request while it runs.** A message you type while Nowa AI is working waits at the top of the chat field, marked **Queued**, until Nowa AI picks it up at its next step. Click **×** to take it back.

Chats take turns changing your project, and each one keeps its own checkpoints. See [Chat with Nowa AI](/ai/chat).

## 🎚️ Pick the mode and how hard Nowa AI thinks, in one place

The mode chip under the chat field now shows both choices, like **Agent · Thinking**. Click it to pick **Design**, **Plan** or **Agent**, then drag the slider under them to choose **Instant** for quick edits, **Thinking** for most requests or **Deep Thinking** for big features and tricky logic. The same menu is in the dashboard's **What do you want to build?** box, and Plan mode now gets a thinking level too.

**Thinking** and **Deep Thinking** are part of every paid plan. On the free plan, Nowa AI runs at **Instant**, and a lock on the slider marks the other two. See [Design, Plan and Agent modes](/ai/modes#set-the-thinking-level).

## 🌿 Git for every plan

You can now keep a history of your app on any plan. Open the **Git** panel to see what changed, compare versions, commit, browse your history, and undo or revert a commit. Connecting GitHub to push, pull and work with branches is still part of the paid plans. See [Use Git](/code/git).

Git also keeps up with the rest of your work. Files that come in with a pull, a branch switch or a merge show up right away, and when something changes outside Nowa, Nowa reloads the project once and tells you why. It never reloads while you have unsaved edits.

## 📊 Credits you can follow

- **A meter that keeps up.** The usage meter under the chat moves after every step Nowa AI takes, in every chat that's running.
- **Deep Thinking goes the distance.** It no longer stops early saying your budget is almost used up. It keeps working until your credits really run out.
- **Back as soon as you are.** After the reset or a top-up, **Send** comes back by itself, and **Continue** picks up where Nowa AI left off.

See [Plans, billing and AI usage](/account/plans-and-usage#usage).

## 📱 Cloud builds that keep working

When Flutter released a new version on 11 November, many Android cloud builds stopped working. Now each project builds with the Flutter and Xcode versions its Nowa release was made for, so a new Flutter or Xcode release won't break a build that used to work. When you open a project, Nowa changes its Android files only if they're too old to build.

If your app uses a **Web View**, Nowa asks once to move it into your project, with a **Web View update** notice that lists each change. Click **Migrate**, and your screens look and work the same. Apps without a web view get lighter.
